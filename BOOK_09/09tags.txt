H1|Part 1: Deep Work aur Shallow Work ka Core Concept
H2|1. Deep Work kya hota hai?
P|Deep Work ka matlab hai aisa kaam jisme aap bina kisi distraction ke apna poora attention ek difficult aur mentally demanding task par lagate hain.
P|Is type ke kaam mein:
B|Aapka poora concentration ek hi important task par hota hai.
B|Aap baar-baar phone, email, social media ya doosri cheezon ki taraf nahi dekhte.
B|Aap apni mental abilities ko maximum level tak use karte hain.
B|Aap nayi aur valuable cheezein create kar sakte hain.
B|Is tarah ka kaam doosre logon ke liye easily copy karna mushkil hota hai.
P|Simple words mein:
P|Deep Work = distraction ko side mein rakhkar difficult aur valuable kaam par gehra focus.
H2|2. Shallow Work kya hota hai?
P|Shallow Work us kaam ko kaha gaya hai jisme zyada mental effort nahi lagta.
P|Examples:
B|Emails check karna
B|Messages ka reply dena
B|Internet surfing

P|Iska main problem yeh hai ki yeh generally nayi ya highly valuable value create nahi karta aur isse replace ya copy karna comparatively easy hota hai.
