H1|Book Ka Parichay
P|Chet Holmes ki famous book "The Ultimate Sales Machine" ka yeh bahut detailed aur comprehensive content hai. Chet Holmes kehte hain ki business mein "Mastery" koi special talent ya gift nahi hai. Yeh "pigheaded discipline", yani bahut ziddi discipline, aur determination ka seedha result hai.
P|Business ko ek machine ki tarah chalane ke liye basic cheezon ko baar-baar repeat karna padta hai, jab tak woh naturally aapki aadat ka hissa na ban jayein.
P|Is content ko 4 main parts mein divide kiya gaya hai: Management, Marketing, Sales, aur Operating Your Ultimate Sales Machine, yani USM.
H1|Part 1: Management
P|Management mein Chet Holmes ne organization ko andar se strong banane aur productivity ko maximum karne ke tareeqe bataye hain.
H2|Great Time Management: Waqt Ka Sahi Istemal
P|Aapko aur aapki team ko achanak aane wale issues par sirf react karne ke bajaye apne time par control paana hoga. Iske liye Chet Holmes ne 6 important steps diye hain jo sirf 5 minutes mein kiye ja sakte hain.

S|Staff ko kahe ki emails ke titles clear aur descriptive rakhein, taaki aap sirf wahi emails open karein jinhe turant handle karna hai.
B|Daily 6 Important Tasks Ki List Banayein: Apne din ke 6 sabse important tasks likhein aur unhe poora karne ka pakka decision lein.
B|Time Allocate Karein: Har task ke liye pehle se time slot fix karein.
S|Ideal tareeqe se in 6 tasks ke liye total 6 hours schedule karein.
S|Agar koi task bada hai, toh use chhote parts mein divide karein.
B|Difficult Tasks Ko Pehle Karein: Sabse important aur mushkil kaam sabse pehle schedule karein, jab aapki energy sabse zyada ho.
B|Business Growth Ke Liye Dedicated Time: Agar aap salesperson hain ya akele team chala rahe hain, toh roz kam se kam 2.5 hours sirf business ko grow karne mein lagayein.
B|Faltu Files Ko Hata Dein: Lagbhag 80% stored files dobara kabhi use nahi hoti hain.
S|Khud se poochein, "Kya ise phenkne se koi nuksan hoga?"
S|Jo zaroori nahi hai use hata dein.

H2|Workshop Meetings: Company Ko Improve Karne Ka Tareeqa

B|Workshop Structure: Har workshop mein maximum 30 log hone chahiye.

S|Sabse high score wale top 5 issues ko priority dein.
S|Har issue par alag workshop karein.
B|Create Manuals: Har session ke baad solutions ko 1 ya 2 page ke memos mein likhein.

H2|Implementing New Policies Effectively: Nayi Policy Lagu Karne Ke 10 Steps

H2|Hiring and Managing Superstars

B|30%: Yeh log interested nahi hain, yani Not Interested.
B|30%: Yeh log puri tarah disinterested hain aur apni current situation, yani status quo, ko badalna nahi chahte.
