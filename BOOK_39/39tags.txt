H1|Ghadisaaz Banna, Waqt Batana Nahi — Clock-Building, Not Time-Telling
B|Myths Debunked — Bharm ka Nivaran: Kitab do bade bharm door karti hai. Pehla, ek badi company shuru karne ke liye kisi bahut bade ya "great idea" ki zaroorat hoti hai. Doosra, iske liye ek charismatic aur bahut attractive leader ka hona zaroori hai.
B|Buniyaad Par Focus: Visionary companies ki long-term success kisi ek special idea ya charismatic leader ke powerful personality par depend nahi karti. Unki success ka secret unki organization ki strong foundations mein hota hai. Yeh foundations leaders aur product life-cycles se bhi aage tak chalti hain. Asli creation khud company hoti hai, sirf uske products ya leaders nahi.
B|Misaalein — Examples: 18 visionary companies mein se sirf 3 companies aisi thi jo kisi bade aur innovative idea se shuru hui thin.
S|HP ke founders ne shuruat mein automatic urinal flushers aur fat-reduction shock machines tak banane ki koshish ki thi.
S|3M ki shuruat itni mushkil thi ki uske doosre president ne pehle 11 saal tak koi salary nahi li.
S|Kitab kehti hai ki jo companies shuruat mein slow hoti hain, woh aksar long run mein jeet jaati hain.
B|Leaders Ka Kirdaar: Charismatic leaders achhe ho sakte hain, lekin zaroori nahi hote. Kai visionary leaders, jaise 3M ke William McKnight, Sony ke Masaru Ibuka, HP ke Bill Hewlett aur Boeing ke Bill Allen, bilkul high-profile ya charismatic nahi the.
S|Clock Builders: Yeh leaders aisi organization design karne par focus karte the jo unke baad bhi chalti rahe.
S|Time-Tellers: Iske opposite, woh leaders jo mainly apna influence aur dabdaba banaye rakhne par focus karte hain.
H1|Munafa Se Badhkar — More Than Profits
B|Core Ideology — Mool Vichardhara: Core Ideology = Core Values + Purpose. Visionary companies ke liye paisa kamana hi sab kuch nahi hota. Profit unke purpose ka ek hissa hota hai.
S|Profit ki Role: Profit oxygen, khana, paani aur khoon ki tarah hai. Yeh life ke liye zaroori hain, lekin life ka purpose nahi ho sakte.
B|"AND" Ka Karishma — Genius of the "AND" vs "Tyranny of the OR": Visionary companies khud ko sirf ek option tak limit nahi karti, jaise stability OR change ya low cost OR high quality. Woh dono cheezon ko ek saath apnati hain.
S|Munafa BHI aur Maksad BHI — profits AND purpose.
S|Purani parampara BHI aur bold kadam BHI — conservative AND bold.
S|Strong ideological control BHI aur employees ki freedom BHI — ideological control AND autonomy.
B|Mool Mulya — Core Values: Organization ke paas 3 se 6 genuine core values, yani enduring tenets, hote hain jinhe woh paison ya short-term fayde ke liye kabhi compromise nahi karti.
S|Har company ke apne alag core values ho sakte hain. Koi ek universal "sahi" core value nahi hoti.
S|Important yeh hai ki company apni values par kitna gehra belief rakhti hai aur un values ko practically kaise jeeti hai.
B|Core Purpose — Mool Maksad: Paisa kamane se pare company ke existence ka main reason kya hai, ise Core Purpose kehte hain.
S|Marriott ka purpose hai ki logon ko ghar se door hone par bhi aisa feel ho ki woh doston ke beech hain aur log unhe genuinely pasand karte hain.
S|Purpose kabhi completely finish nahi hota. Yeh hamesha company ko aage badhne ki motivation deta rehta hai.
B|Words Nahi, Deeds: Sabse important cheez sahi alignment aur authenticity hai. Visionary companies yeh nahi poochti ki "humein kya value karna chahiye", balki yeh poochti hain ki "hum andar se sach mein kis cheez ko value karte hain".
H1|Core Ko Surakshit Rakhna, Pragati Ko Badhava Dena — Preserve the Core, Stimulate Progress
B|Yin aur Yang Ka Rishta: Core ideology ko protect karna aur progress ki strong desire rakhna, dono ek doosre ke complementary parts hain. Sirf change hi sab kuch nahi hai. Core ideology stable rehti hai, jabki baaki operating practices badalti rehti hain.
B|Pragati ki Tadap — Drive for Progress: Yeh andar ki woh strong energy hai jo new discoveries, change aur improvement ke liye push karti hai.
S|Self-confidence: Bade aur difficult goals set karne ka confidence.
S|Self-criticism: Bahar ki duniya ke kehne se pehle khud mein improvement karna.
B|Core vs Non-Core: 3M ka "respect for individual initiative" unka core value hai aur yeh change nahi hota.
S|Non-Core Practice: Employees ko apne pasand ke projects par 15% time kaam karne dena ek non-core practice hai, jise badla ya improve kiya ja sakta hai.
B|Concrete Mechanisms: Visionary companies apni ideology ko practical level par lane ke liye tangible mechanisms banati hain.
S|Walt Disney ne new employees ki training ke liye Disney University banayi aur "Disney Traditions" seminars ko compulsory kiya.
H1|Tangible Mechanisms Banane Ke 5 Tarike
P|Visionary companies apni core ideology aur progress ki drive ko in 5 tarikon se practically apply karti hain.
H2|Way 1: Big Hairy Audacious Goals — BHAGs
B|Bade aur Sahasi Lakshya: BHAGs, jise "bee-hags" bola jata hai, progress ko stimulate karne ka bahut powerful tareeka hain. Yeh goals bahut bade aur challenging hote hain, lekin inke liye sabka commitment bahut strong hota hai.
B|Misaal — Example: General Electric (GE) ka BHAG tha: "har us market mein #1 ya #2 banna jisme hum kaam karte hain, aur company ko ek chhote enterprise jaisi tezi aur lachilapan dena".
B|Achhe BHAGs ki Pehchan: Achhe BHAGs clear aur compelling hote hain aur unhe samjhane ke liye zyada explanation ki zaroorat nahi hoti.
S|Yeh company ki core ideology ke saath aligned hote hain.
S|Ek BHAG complete hone ke baad use ek naye BHAG se replace kiya jata hai.
S|Yeh company ke comfort zone se bahut bahar hote hain.
S|Inmein ek "hubris factor" hota hai, yani "hum yeh kar sakte hain" wala strong confidence.
S|Yeh itne bade hote hain ki leaders ke chale jaane ke baad bhi zinda rehte hain.
H2|Way 2: Cult-like Cultures — Cult Jaisi Sanskriti
B|Har Kisi Ke Liye Nahi: Kitab clearly batati hai ki visionary companies sabhi logon ke liye kaam karne ki best jagah zaroori nahi hoti. Inki culture itni strong aur clear hoti hai ki jo log inki ideology ke saath fit hote hain, unke liye yeh jannat jaisi hoti hai. Jo fit nahi hote, unhe jaldi bahar kar diya jata hai. Yahan middle ground nahi hota — ya toh aap andar hain ya bahar.
B|3 Bade Tarike — Mechanisms: Is culture ko strong banane ke teen main tareeke hain.
S|Indoctrination: Bahut strict orientation, training, internal universities, special language aur past success ki stories ke through logon ko company ki ideology ke hisaab se dhalna.
S|Tight Fit: Recruitment ke time strict screening karna aur advancement aur incentives ko core values ke saath jodna.
S|Elitism: Apne aap ko special aur doosron se alag samajhne ki feeling ko encourage karna.
B|Nordstrom Ka Example: Nordies, yani Nordstrom ke employees, ko bahut deep training di jati hai.
S|Unka organizational chart ulta hota hai, jisme customers aur sales support top par hote hain aur Board of Directors sabse neeche hota hai.
S|Customer service ke liye bahut strict standards hote hain aur top achievers ko achhe rewards milte hain.
H2|Way 3: Experiment A Lot and Keep What Works — Bahut Prayog Karein aur Jo Kaam Kare Use Rakhein
B|Bhool aur Sudhaar — Trial & Error: Yeh ek myth hai ki har successful cheez bahut brilliant aur complex strategic planning se hi hoti hai. Reality yeh hai ki visionary companies ke kai best decisions trial-and-error, opportunism aur achanak hue accidents se nikle.
B|Example: Johnson & Johnson (J&J) ne consumer products mein tab entry ki jab unka talcum powder, jo woh medicated plasters ke saath dete the, customers ko bahut pasand aaya aur hit ho gaya.
B|Evolutionary Progress: Yeh nature ke evolution ki tarah hai, jahan dheere-dheere chhote changes hote rehte hain.
S|Company control BHI rakhti hai aur employees ko kaam karne aur naye experiments karne ki operational autonomy, yani kaafi freedom, BHI deti hai.
B|3M Ke Rules: 3M ke paas naye products develop karne ke liye special rules hain.
S|"15% rule": Employees apna 15% time apni pasand ke projects par laga sakte hain.
S|"25% rule": Har division ko apni sales ka 25% hissa pichle 5 saal mein banaye gaye naye products se lana hota hai.
S|"Own business opportunities": Naye products ko khud chalane aur business opportunity banane ka mauka dena.
B|Evolution Ke Mool Mantra: Hamesha move karte rahein, yani try karein aur adjust karein; mistakes ko accept karein; chhote-chhote steps lein; logon ko freedom dein; aur core ko hamesha protect karein.
H2|Way 4: Home-Grown Management — Ghar Ka Management
B|Andar Se Leadership: Yeh myth hai ki naye changes lane ke liye bahar se kisi naye CEO ko lana zaroori hota hai. Visionary companies apne insiders ko promote karti hain. Isse unka core protected rehta hai aur leadership ki continuity bani rehti hai.
B|Bemisaal Ankde — Data: Visionary companies ke 1700 saal ke combined history mein sirf 4 baar kisi outsider ko chief executive banaya gaya.
B|Leadership Continuity Loop: Management development aur succession planning se leadership ki continuity bani rehti hai. Is process se strong internal candidates ready hote hain jo aage promote kiye ja sakte hain.
H2|Way 5: Good Enough Never Is — Behtar Kabhi Kaafi Nahi Hota
B|Khud Se Muqabla: Visionary companies doosron ko harane ke bajay khud ko beat karne aur apne previous performance se behtar banne par focus karti hain. Continuous improvement unki habit aur way of life hoti hai.
B|Complacency Se Ladna: Market leader banne ke baad jo aalas ya self-satisfaction aa sakti hai, use door rakhne ke liye yeh companies khud ke liye discomfort create karti hain aur strict self-discipline maintain karti hain.
B|Misaalein — Examples: Is approach ko kuch companies is tarah apply karti hain.
S|P&G apne hi brands ko ek-doosre ke against compete karwata hai taaki woh aapas mein competition karein.
S|Boeing "eyes of the enemy" planning process use karta hai. Isme managers dushman company ki tarah sochkar Boeing ko harane ki strategies banate hain aur phir un strategies ka tod nikalte hain.
S|SPH customer feedback ko directly employee compensation se connect karta hai.
B|Lambi Soch: Yeh companies human capital aur future technologies mein bahut bada long-term investment karti hain.
H1|Anth Se Shuruat: Sahi Alignment Banana — The End at the Beginning & Alignment
B|Sirf Vision Statement Se Kuch Nahi Hota: Sirf ek bada vision statement bana dena enough nahi hota. Sabse difficult aur important kaam us vision ko company ke har part, goal, strategy aur culture mein align karna hota hai.
H2|Alignment Ke Guideposts
B|Paint the Whole Picture — Poori Tasveer Sajayein: Company ke sabhi signals aur actions ek-doosre ko strong banana chahiye.
B|Sweat the Small Stuff — Chhoti Baaton Par Dhyan Dein: Roz ke chhote-chhote actions hi logon ke trust ko strong banate hain.
B|Cluster, Don't Shotgun: Naye methods aur mechanisms ko alag-alag direction mein na rakhein. Unhe ek saath align karein taaki woh ek-doosre ko reinforce karein.
B|Apne Compass Par Chalein — Own Internal Compass: Duniya mein jo practices "good" ya "bad" mani jaati hain, unke peeche blindly na bhagein. Wahi karein jo aapki company ke liye fit baithta hai.
B|Misalignments Ko Khatam Karein: Jo cheezein company ki core ideology ke against ja rahi hain, unhe bina hesitation ke hata dein.
B|Naye Tareeqe Khojein Par Universal Rules Sthir Rakhein: Core Ideology, Drive for Progress aur is core ko protect karne wala strong design hamesha bana rehna chahiye.

B|Envisioned Future: Yeh future ki ek concrete aur vivid picture hoti hai. Isme ek vision-level BHAG hota hai aur ek strong Vivid Description hoti hai jo batati hai ki goal achieve hone ke baad life kaisi dikhegi.
