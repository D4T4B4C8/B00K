Reading notes on Ultralearning

12 min read•By Scott H. Young • Added August 2026

Scott H. Young ki book "Ultralearning" mushkil skills ko jaldi seekhne, career grow karne aur personal interests ko master karne ke liye ek powerful aur intense learning strategy batati hai. Iska main idea hai ki agar aap learning ko sahi tareeke se plan karke, focused practice aur real-world application ke saath karein, to aap kam time mein bahut kuch seekh sakte hain.

1. Introduction aur "The Big So What?"

The Big Question

Kya aap bina college ya MIT jaaye MIT-level ki education le sakte ho? Kya aap sirf 3 mahine mein kisi foreign language mein fluent ho sakte ho?

Book ka answer hai: Haan, mumkin hai.

Duniya ki Demand

Aaj ki fast aur competitive economy mein jo log nayi skills ko jaldi nahi seekh paate, woh peeche reh sakte hain.

Ultralearning aapko kam time mein naye fields mein grow karne aur mastery ki taraf badhne mein help karti hai. Ye professional career ke liye bhi useful hai aur personal interests, jaise photography ya cooking skills, ke liye bhi.

2. Ultralearning Kya Hai?

Definition

Ultralearning ek aisi strategy hai jisme aap khud apni learning ko direct karte ho aur bahut focused aur intense tareeke se skills aur knowledge seekhte ho.

Iska goal hai:

Deep learning karna.

Focused practice karna.

Apni limits ko push karna.

Kam se kam time mein strong results paana.

Scott Young ka MIT Challenge

Scott Young computer science seekhna chahte the, lekin MIT ki expensive fees afford nahi kar sakte the.

Unhone MIT ke free online courses ko research kiya, final exams ko target banaya aur backward planning ki. Sirf 1 saal mein unhone MIT ke computer science program ke saare final exams pass kar liye.

Iske baad:

12 mahine mein 4 languages seekhi.

Sirf 30 din mein portrait drawing seekhi.

Dusre Ultralearners

Scott Young ne polyglots, yani kai languages bolne wale logon, math geniuses aur game-show winners ko study kiya. Unhone in logon ke common patterns se Ultralearning ke 9 core principles identify kiye.

3. The 9 Ultralearning Principles

Principle 1: Metalearning — Start with a Road Map

Metalearning ka simple matlab hai: "Learning ko kaise seekhna hai."

Ye aapke learning journey ka roadmap hai. Isse aap bina direction ke idhar-udhar bhatakne se bach sakte ho.

3 Sawal:

Why (Kyon): Sabse pehle decide karo ki aap ye skill kyon seekhna chahte ho. Isse aapko pata chalega ki aapko exactly kis type ki knowledge aur practice chahiye. Experts se baat karna useful ho sakta hai — social media par experts ko 15-minute video call ke liye request ki ja sakti hai.

What (Kya): Ek paper par 3 columns banao — Concepts, Facts, aur Procedures.

Concepts: Kaunse bade ideas samajhne hain? Example: Maths mein symbols aur equations.

Facts: Kya cheezein yaad karni hain? Example: Vocabulary ya formulas.

Procedures: Kaunse physical ya mental actions practice karne hain? Example: Coding likhna.

Yahin par apne bottlenecks, yani main rukawaton, ko identify karo.

How (Kaise): Ab decide karo ki skill ko kaise seekhna hai.

Benchmarking: Research karo ki schools ya textbooks normally is subject ko kaise sikhate hain.

Modify Curriculum: Us standard curriculum mein se un cheezon ko hatao ya badlo jo aapke personal goal ke liye useful nahi hain. Example: Agar aapko 2 hafton mein Paris jaana hai, to French spelling par zyada focus karne ke bajaye speaking par focus karna zyada useful ho sakta hai.

The 10% Rule

Apne total learning time ka lagbhag 10% starting research aur planning mein invest karo.

Learning ke beech mein bhi progress check karte raho. Dekho ki kahin learning ki speed slow to nahi ho rahi.

Principle 2: Focus — Concentration ki Ability Strong Karo

Concentrated learning se best results milte hain.

Focus ke 3 bade obstacles aur unke solutions:

Procrastination (Kaam ko Taalna): Jab hum kaam shuru karne wale hote hain, dimaag excuses dhoondhne lagta hai.

Apne andar ke dar ko accept karo.

Khud se bolo ki sirf 5–10 minutes kaam karna hai.

Ek baar start karne ke baad dimaag dheere-dheere settle ho sakta hai.

Rule: Har 25 minutes ke focused work ke baad 5 minutes ka break lo. Aur jab tak ek clear milestone, jaise koi important information yaad karna, complete na ho, tab tak quit na karne ka rule banao.

Distractions (Dhyan Bhatakna): Examples — Daydreaming, baar-baar phone check karna.

Ek learning environment banao jahan TV aur phone distractions kam hon.

Aisa medium choose karo jo attention hold kare, jaise audio, video ya notes banana.

Gussa aur frustration jaise negative emotions ko forcefully suppress karne ke bajaye unhe calmly pass hone do.

Phir dobara focus par aao.

Variety / Mental Arousal: Simple tasks ke liye high-energy environment aur upbeat music useful ho sakta hai. Example: Darts throw karna. Lekin complex tasks, jaise maths problem solve karna, ke liye quiet aur relaxed environment zyada useful ho sakta hai.

Principle 3: Directness — Seedha Application Mein Jao

Jis real-world situation mein aap skill ko use karna chahte ho, usi situation mein directly practice karna sabse useful hai.

Schools aksar theoretical knowledge classroom mein dete hain, lekin real world mein us knowledge ko apply karna mushkil ho sakta hai.

Ek architecture graduate ko job nahi mil rahi thi. Usne directly software seekha, khud building design ki aur us portfolio ki help se job haasil ki.

Jeopardy! champion Roger Craig ne simulated practice ke liye purane game shows ka use kiya.

Project-Based Learning: Coding seekhni hai to directly ek game banao.

Immersive Learning: Language seekhni hai to us country ke logon ke beech rehkar practice karo.

Simulations: Flight ya surgery simulators ka use karo.

Overkill: Apne comfort zone se bahar jaakar high-standard situations mein practice karo. Example: Public speaking seekhni hai to kisi speaking contest mein participate karo.

Principle 4: Drill — Apni Sabse Badi Weakness par Kaam Karo

Complex skill mein aksar ek weak component aapki overall speed ko slow karta hai. Is weak point ko bottleneck kaha ja sakta hai.

Us bottleneck ko alag karke repeatedly practice karna Drill hai.

Direct-Then-Drill Strategy

Pehle skill ko directly practice karo.

Apni sabse badi weakness identify karo.

Us weakness ko alag karo.

Us part ki repeatedly intense practice karo.

Phir direct practice par wapas aao aur check karo ki drill useful thi ya nahi.

Drill Design Tips

Time Slicing: Badi activity ke difficult chhote part ko alag karke practice karo. Example: Piano music ke sabse difficult bars ko baar-baar bajana.

Cognitive Components: Mental components ko separately practice karo. Example: Words ke pairs ko different tones mein pronounce karke record karna.

Copying — Franklin's Method: Benjamin Franklin apni writing improve karne ke liye magazine ke arguments ko yaad karte, phir dobara likhte aur original ke saath compare karke apni mistakes improve karte the.

Apne goals ko yaad rakhkar khud ko motivated rakho.

Principle 5: Retrieval — Self-Testing se Seekho

Book ko baar-baar passive tareeke se padhne ke bajaye, book band karke memory se information recall karne ki koshish karo.

Retrieval practice active learning ko strong banati hai.

Desirable Difficulty

Psychologist R.A. Bjork ne "Desirable Difficulty" term di.

Jab brain ko information yaad karne ke liye thoda effort karna padta hai, to memory zyada strong ho sakti hai.

Lekin test itna difficult nahi hona chahiye ki aapko kuch bhi yaad na aaye. Difficulty itni honi chahiye ki brain stretch ho.

Forward-Testing

Class shuru hone se pehle hi final exam dena.

Isse mind important details notice karne ke liye zyada prepared ho sakta hai.

Retrieval Methods

Flashcards: Vocabulary aur definitions ko pair karne ke liye.

Free Recall: Lecture ya chapter ke baad blank paper par jo kuch yaad hai, bina book dekhe likho.

Q&A Notes: Notes ko questions aur answers ke format mein banao.

Closed-Book Concept Maps: Book dekhe bina poore chapter ka mind map banao.

Principle 6: Feedback — Punches se Mat Bhago

Feedback uncomfortable ho sakta hai, lekin constructive criticism se bhaagna nahi chahiye.

Apne ego ko side par rakho aur useful signal ko unnecessary noise se alag karna seekho.

Chris Rock Example: Chris Rock bina announce kiye Comedy Cellar jaakar small crowds ke saamne naye jokes test karte hain. Isse unhe immediate aur genuine feedback milta hai.

Feedback tabhi useful hota hai jab woh future action ko guide kare.

Personal comments jaise "tum smart ho" ya "tum stupid ho" learning ko improve karne ke bajaye damage kar sakte hain.

3 Types of Feedback

Outcome Feedback: Ye batata hai ki result kaisa raha. Example: Pass/fail ya A/C grade. Ye motivate kar sakta hai, lekin ye zaroori nahi batata ki kya change karna hai.

Informational Feedback: Ye batata hai ki kya galat hua, lekin ye zaroori nahi batata ki use kaise fix karna hai. Example: Audience ka chup rehna. Agar feedback immediate ho, to aap naye solutions test kar sakte ho. Example: Programmer error code ko baar-baar run karta hai jab tak error fix na ho.

Corrective Feedback: Ye sabse useful type hai. Ye batata hai ki kya galat hai aur use kaise fix karna hai. Iske liye coach ya mentor useful ho sakta hai.

Tactics

Noise filter karo. Example: Blog success ko sirf visitors se measure mat karo. Click rate aur scroll depth jaise useful signals bhi dekho.

High-intensity feedback seek karo aur aise level par kaam karo jahan success aur failure dono ka meaningful chance ho.

Principle 7: Retention — Knowledge ko Leak Hone se Roko

Hamare brain ko ek leaky bucket ki tarah samjha ja sakta hai. Agar knowledge ko review na kiya jaye, to nayi information dheere-dheere bhool sakte hain.

Forgetting Curve

Hermann Ebbinghaus ne dikhaya ki conscious review ke bina hum kuch hi dino mein kaafi information bhool sakte hain.

Bhoolne ke 3 Reasons

Memory Decay: Time ke saath memories weak ho sakti hain.

Interference: Purani aur nayi knowledge ek doosre ke saath interfere kar sakti hain.

Proactive interference: Purani knowledge nayi knowledge ko yaad karne mein problem create karti hai.

Retroactive interference: Nayi knowledge purani knowledge ko yaad karna difficult bana sakti hai.

Forgotten Cues: Information memory mein ho sakti hai, lekin use recall karne ke cues ya signals kho sakte hain.

4 Retention Mechanisms

Spaced Repetition: 10 ghante ek hi din mein cramming karne ke bajaye, 10 din tak roz 1 ghanta padho. Review intervals ko gradually increase karo.

Daily

Weekly

Monthly

Proceduralization: Skills ko clear step-by-step actions mein convert karo. Examples: Typing ya swimming.

Overlearning: Skill perfect hone ke baad bhi practice continue karo taaki skill long-term memory mein strong ho.

Mnemonics: Difficult information ko vivid images, associations ya maps mein convert karo.

Principle 8: Intuition — Deep Jao, Phir Build Up Karo

Real mastery sirf rote learning se nahi aati. Deep understanding aur strong mental models banana zaroori hai.

Intuition ke 4 Rules

Mushkil problems par jaldi give up mat karo. Agar stuck ho, to kam se kam 10 minutes aur push karne ki koshish karo.

Principles ko khud prove ya replicate karo. Example: Bicycle ka drawing bana kar explain karna ki gears kaise connect hote hain.

Abstract ideas ke bajaye concrete examples se shuru karo.

Experts se "dumb" questions puchne se mat daro. Hum aksar ye nahi jaante ki hum kya nahi jaante. Isliye basic questions bhi useful ho sakte hain.

The Feynman Technique

Richard Feynman ki learning approach se inspired technique:

Paper ke top par concept ka naam likho.

Concept ko aise explain karo jaise aap kisi beginner ko sikha rahe ho.

Simple words use karo aur unnecessary technical jargon avoid karo.

Agar explanation ke beech mein atak jao, to dobara source padho.

Phir concept ko aur simple tareeke se explain karo.

Ye process aapke knowledge gaps ko clearly dikha deta hai.

Principle 9: Experimentation — Comfort Zone se Bahar Explore Karo

Jaise-jaise aap expert bante ho, aapko guide karne wale log kam ho sakte hain.

True mastery ke liye kabhi-kabhi purani assumptions ko question karna, kuch unlearn karna aur naye options try karna zaroori hota hai.

Vincent van Gogh Example: Van Gogh ne 26 saal ki age tak painting start nahi ki thi. Unhone books aur doosre artists ko study kiya, strong feedback liya aur thick paint aur bold colors jaise extremes ke saath experiments kiye. In experiments ne unki unique signature style develop karne mein help ki.

3 Types of Experimentation

Resources: Different books, online courses aur learning environments ko try karo.

Techniques: Kisi particular sub-topic ya specialization mein deep dive karo. Example: Language learning mein sirf comics ya business literature padhna.

Styles: Masters ke style ko study karo aur unke methods ko copy, emulate ya apne work mein integrate karo.

Experiment Kaise Karein?

Copy Then Create: Starting mein doosron ke work ko copy karke basic understanding banao. Phir apna original work create karo.

Split-Testing: Do different methods ko side-by-side compare karo.

Introduce Constraints: Nayi limits set karo jo aapko naye solutions dhoondhne par majboor karein.

Combine Unrelated Skills: Do different skills ko combine karke unique advantage banao. Example: Scott Adams ne Dilbert comic ke liye engineering aur cartooning ko combine kiya.

Explore the Extremes: Sirf safe middle ground par mat raho. Kabhi-kabhi extremes ko test karo aur phir jo best kaam kare uski taraf wapas aao.

5. Project ke Baad Kya Karein?

Project complete hone ke baad 3 main options hain:

Maintain: Practice ki frequency decide karo aur skill ko apni life mein integrate karo.

Relearn: Agar future mein skill bhool bhi jao, to use dobara seekhna scratch se seekhne ke comparison mein easier ho sakta hai.

Master: Skill mein aur deep jao ya us knowledge ko kisi new domain mein transfer karo.

Conclusion

Ultralearning sirf ek learning method nahi hai. Ye ek growth mindset bhi hai.

Ye aapko rapidly changing aur uncertain environments mein adapt karna, naye skills seekhna aur mastery ki taraf badhna sikhata hai.

Aap apni learning ke results ke liye khud responsible hote ho.

Is approach ka sabse bada benefit ye hai ki ye aapke liye naye opportunities aur naye possibilities ke doors khol sakti hai.

Core Message

Agar aap kisi difficult skill ko fast aur deeply seekhna chahte ho:

Pehle roadmap banao.

Focus ko protect karo.

Skill ko real life mein directly practice karo.

Apni weakest point ko drill karo.

Khud ko test karke retrieval practice karo.

Strong feedback lo.

Spaced repetition se knowledge retain karo.

Deep understanding develop karo.

Experiments karte raho.

Project ke baad skill ko maintain, relearn ya master karo.

Ultralearning ka main idea hai: learning ko passive activity mat banao. Khud direction set karo, focused practice karo, real situations mein apply karo, mistakes se feedback lo aur continuously improve karte raho.
