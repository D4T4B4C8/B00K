B="Blue Ocean Strategy: How to Create Uncontested Market Space And Make the Competition Irrelevant"
A="W. Chan Kim aur Renée Mauborgne"
H1|Kitab ka Parichay
P|Yeh book Blue Ocean Strategy ke baare mein hai. Iska focus yeh samajhna hai ki company existing competition mein ladne ke bajaye ek aisa naya market space kaise bana sakti hai jahan direct competition na ho.
B|Research Basis: Yeh kitab 150 strategic moves par ki gayi study par based hai. Yeh study 100 saal se zyada ke time aur 30 alag industries ko cover karti hai.
B|Core Philosophy: Traditional tareeqe se competitors ko harane ke bajaye ek aisa uncontested market space banana chahiye jahan competition ka importance hi khatam ho jaye. Blue Oceans naye type ki demand create karte hain, jisse sales, profits aur growth ke naye aur unexplored opportunities milti hain.
H1|Red Ocean aur Blue Ocean ka Antar
P|Book mein market ko do parts mein samjhaya gaya hai: Red Oceans aur Blue Oceans.
H2|Red Ocean Strategy
B|Existing market space mein compete karna: Pehle se maujood market mein hi competition karna.
B|Beat the competition: Competitors ko harane par focus karna.
B|Exploit existing demand: Jo demand pehle se maujood hai, usi ko capture karne ki koshish karna.
B|Make the value-cost trade-off: Quality badhane ke liye cost badhana ya cost kam karne ke liye quality se compromise karna.
B|Pursue low-cost OR differentiation: Ya toh low cost par kaam karna ya kuch alag offer karna; dono ko ek saath pursue nahi kiya jata.
H2|Blue Ocean Strategy
B|Create uncontested market space: Ek naya aur khali market space banana jahan koi doosra competitor na ho.
B|Make the competition irrelevant: Competition ko be-asar bana dena, kyunki aapka seedha mukabla kisi se nahi hota.
B|Create and capture new demand: Naye customers aur nayi demand paida karna aur us demand ko capture karna.
B|Break the value-cost trade-off: Value badhane aur cost kam karne ke beech jo purana compromise hai, use todna.
B|Pursue differentiation AND low cost: Ek hi waqt mein low cost aur differentiation, yani alag aur behtar value, dono hasil karna.
H1|Value Innovation: Blue Ocean Strategy ki Buniyad
B|Definition: Value Innovation Blue Ocean Strategy ka sabse important foundation hai. Yeh tab hota hai jab company ke actions se cost kam hoti hai aur saath hi buyers ke liye value offering badhti hai.
B|Process: Company un factors ko eliminate ya reduce karke cost kam karti hai jinki industry mein zarurat nahi hai. Saath hi naye factors ko raise ya create karke buyer ke liye value badhati hai.
B|Key Insight: Kim aur Mauborgne kehte hain ki competitors ko harane ke bajaye buyers aur company dono ke liye value ka ek bada leap create karna chahiye. Yeh sirf innovation nahi hai. Yeh aisi strategy hai jo company ki poori activities ke system ko badal deti hai.
H1|Analytical Tools and Frameworks
P|Book mein Blue Ocean Strategy ko formulate aur execute karne ke liye naye aur practical tools diye gaye hain.
H2|The Strategy Canvas
B|Purpose: Strategy Canvas ek aisa tool hai jo market ki current situation ko capture karta hai, taaki samjha ja sake ki competitors kahan sabse zyada invest kar rahe hain, industry mein products, service aur delivery ke through kis tarah compete kiya ja raha hai, aur customers ko competitors se abhi kya mil raha hai.
B|Strategic Shift: Ismein focus competitors se hata kar alternatives par aur existing customers se hata kar noncustomers par kiya jata hai. Iska aim industry boundaries ke bahar naye buyer value elements dhoondhna hai.
B|BOS Curve Rules: Aapka strategy curve aisa hona chahiye jo competitors se clearly alag dikhe.
S|Industry ke purane aur unnecessary competition factors ko cut back ya eliminate kare, jisse cost kam ho.
S|Aise naye factors ko raise ya create kare jo industry abhi offer nahi karti, jisse differentiation badhe.
B|Good Strategy ke 3 Components: Ek achhi strategy mein Focus, Divergence aur Tagline ke teen components hone chahiye.
S|Focus: Apne saare efforts ko har factor par kharch na karein. Kuch main factors par focus rakhein.
S|Divergence: Aapka value curve competitors ke curve se clearly alag dikhna chahiye.
S|Tagline: Aisi tagline honi chahiye jo bilkul clear message de aur sach mein aapki offering ko show kare.
H2|Four Action Framework and ERRC Grid
P|Yeh dono tools Strategy Canvas ke saath milkar value-cost trade-off ko todne ke liye use hote hain.
B|Four Action Framework: Is framework mein chaar main sawal pooche jate hain.
S|Eliminate: Industry jin factors ko important maanti hai, unmein se kin factors ko poori tarah khatam kiya ja sakta hai?
S|Reduce: Kin factors ko industry ke standard se bahut kam kiya ja sakta hai?
S|Raise: Kin factors ko industry ke standard se bahut upar uthaya ja sakta hai?
S|Create: Aise kaun se factors hain jo industry ne pehle kabhi offer nahi kiye aur jinhe naye tareeqe se create karna chahiye?
B|ERRC Grid: Yeh grid company ko in chaar sawalon par action lene ke liye push karta hai. Isse ek naya value curve banaya ja sakta hai aur company ke andar us strategy ko communicate kiya ja sakta hai.
H2|Pioneer-Migrator-Settler Map
B|Purpose: Yeh map senior managers ko company ki current aur future offerings ke portfolio ko balance karne mein help karta hai. Portfolio mein teen categories hoti hain.
B|Settlers: Yeh "me-too" products hote hain, yani aise products jo doosron ki nakal hote hain. Inki growth kam hoti hai, lekin yeh cash generate karte hain.
B|Migrators: Yeh market average se behtar hote hain aur customers ko thodi behtar value dete hain.
B|Pioneers: Yeh Value Innovation dete hain, yani aisi value jo pehle is tarah available nahi thi. Yeh company ki Blue Ocean Strategy ke asli drivers aur profitable growth ke sabse bade sources hote hain.
B|Goal: Managers ko current aur future growth ke liye Settlers, Migrators aur Pioneers ka ek balanced portfolio maintain karna chahiye.
H2|Buyer Utility Map
B|Purpose: Buyer Utility Map focus ko customers par shift karta hai, taaki un pain points aur utility spaces ko dhoonda ja sake jinhe company fill kar sakti hai.
B|Buyer Experience Cycle: Customer ka experience cycle 6 stages mein hota hai.
S|Purchase: Product ya service ko kharidna.
S|Delivery: Product ko customer tak pahunchana.
S|Use: Product ya service ka istemal karna.
S|Supplements: Product ke saath zaroori extra accessories ya related cheezein.
S|Maintenance: Product ka rakh-rakhav karna.
S|Disposal: Product ko khatam karna ya use fekna.
B|6 Utility Levers: Yeh 6 factors Buyer Experience Cycle ke stages ke saath kaam karte hain.
S|Customer Productivity: Customer ki productivity badhana.
S|Simplicity: Cheez ko zyada simple aur easy banana.
S|Convenience: Customer ke liye kaam ko zyada convenient banana.
S|Risk: Customer ka risk kam karna.
S|Fun and Image: Customer ke experience aur image ko behtar banana aur use mazedaar banana.
S|Environmental Friendliness: Offering ko environment ke liye zyada friendly banana.
B|Key Insight: Buyer Utility Map se pata chalta hai ki company ki offering ne customer ke poore experience cycle mein sabse bade utility blocks, yani badi pareshaniyon, ko hataya hai ya nahi.
H1|Blue Ocean Strategy Banane ke 4 Key Principles
P|Blue Ocean Strategy ko achhi tarah formulate karne ke liye book mein chaar key principles diye gaye hain.
H2|Principle 1: Reconstruct Market Boundaries
P|Purani market boundaries se bahar nikalne ke liye companies ko chhe paths par kaam karna chahiye.
B|Look across alternative industries: Aise products aur services ko dekhein jinka function alag hai lekin purpose same hai.
S|Example: Theater aur restaurant ka function alag hai, lekin dono entertainment ya outing ke purpose ko serve kar sakte hain.
B|Look across strategic groups within industries: Har industry mein kuch strategic groups hote hain jo similar strategy follow karte hain, jaise luxury cars aur economy cars. In groups ki useful strengths ko combine karein aur baaki unnecessary cheezein eliminate karein.
B|Look across the chain of buyers: Industry aksar kisi ek buyer group par focus karti hai, jaise purchaser, actual user ya influencer. Focus ko shift karke nayi value unlock ki ja sakti hai.
B|Look across complementary product and service offerings: Dekhein ki product use hone se pehle, use hote waqt aur use hone ke baad kya hota hai. In stages ke pain points ko solve karein.
B|Look across functional or emotional appeal to buyers: Agar industry emotional appeal par compete karti hai toh offering ko functional banaya ja sakta hai. Agar industry functional appeal par compete karti hai toh emotional touch diya ja sakta hai.
S|Example: QB House ne traditional hair salon ko zyada functional banaya.
B|Look across time: Aise trends ko dekhein jo irreversible hain aur jinki direction clear hai. In trends ko pehle se use karke market ko shape kiya ja sakta hai.
H2|Principle 2: Reach Beyond Existing Demand
P|Sirf existing customers par focus karne ke bajaye non-consumers ke teen tiers par dhyan dena chahiye, taaki ek bada market create kiya ja sake.
B|First Tier: "Soon-to-be" non-consumers market ke bilkul edge par hote hain. Jaise hi unhe koi better option milta hai, woh existing market ko chhod sakte hain.
B|Second Tier: "Refusing" non-consumers jaan-bujhkar industry ke products ko reject karte hain aur unka use nahi karte.
B|Third Tier: "Unexplored" non-consumers market se sabse door hote hain aur unka market se koi direct connection nahi hota.
B|Action: In teenon groups ki common needs ko samajhkar ek bada customer base create karna chahiye.
H2|Principle 3: Getting the Strategic Sequence Right
P|Ek successful aur commercially viable Blue Ocean idea ke liye neeche diye gaye chaar steps ka sequence sahi hona chahiye aur har step par answer "Yes" hona chahiye.
B|Buyer Utility: Kya aapke idea mein customer ke liye exceptional utility, yani bahut zyada fayda, hai?
B|Price: Kya aapka price aisa hai jo masses, yani aam customers, ke liye easily accessible ho? Price ko Price Corridor ke andar hona chahiye.
B|Cost: Kya aap is price par product bechkar bhi apna profit target cost-efficient tareeqe se achieve kar sakte hain?
B|Adoption: Kya strategy launch karne se pehle adoption hurdles ko address kar liya gaya hai? In hurdles mein employees, partners ya public ka resistance shamil ho sakta hai.
H2|Principle 4: Focus on the Big Picture, Not the Numbers
P|Operational details aur numbers mein zyada ulajhne ke bajaye strategic planning ko Strategy Canvas ke around build karna chahiye. Is process ke chaar components hain.
B|Visual Awakening: Apne business ko competitors ke saath "as is" canvas par draw karke dekhein aur identify karein ki kahan change ki zarurat hai.
B|Visual Exploration: Field mein jaakar 6 paths ko explore karein. Alternative offerings ke benefits aur naye competition factors ko dekhein.
B|Visual Strategy Fair: Field se mili insights ke basis par "to be" strategy canvas banayein. Customers aur non-customers se feedback lein aur sabse behtar strategy choose karein.
B|Visual Communication: "Before-and-after" strategic profile ko ek page par sabhi employees ke beech distribute karein. Sirf unhi projects ko support karein jo strategy ke gap ko fill karte hain.
H1|Blue Ocean Strategy ko Execute Karne ke 2 Key Principles
P|Blue Ocean Strategy existing status quo ko challenge karti hai. Isliye ise successfully execute karne ke liye do key principles bahut important hain.
H2|Principle 1: Overcome Key Organizational Hurdles
P|Iske liye book mein "Tipping Point Leadership" ka concept diya gaya hai. Iska aim kam cost aur kam time mein bade changes lana hai. Yeh chaar main hurdles ko overcome karta hai.
B|Cognitive Hurdle: Logon ko change ki zarurat samajh nahi aati. Sirf numbers dikhane ke bajaye unhe reality ka direct experience karwaya jata hai.
S|Example: Managers ko sabse kharab operational problems dikhayi ja sakti hain ya unhe sabse zyada naraaz customers se milwaya ja sakta hai.
B|Resource Hurdle: Jab resources kam hon, toh naye resources maangne ke bajaye existing resources ki value ko multiply karna chahiye.
S|Hot spots: Resources ko un areas mein lagayein jahan unka impact sabse zyada ho.
S|Cold spots: Aise areas se resources hata dein jahan impact kam ho.
S|Horse-trade: Unused ya kam important resources ko un resources ke saath exchange karein jo zaroori hain.
B|Motivational Hurdle: Key players ko jaldi kaam karne ke liye ready karne ke liye poori company ko motivate karne ke bajaye specific influential people par focus karein.
S|Kingpins: Un influential leaders par focus karein jo organization par strong influence rakhte hain.
S|Fishbowl Management: Kingpins ke actions ko spotlight mein laakar unhe accountable banayein.
S|Atomize: Bade challenge ko chote aur doable parts mein divide karein.
B|Political Hurdle: Jab organization ke andar politics aur opposition ho, toh support aur influence ka sahi use karna chahiye.
S|Angels: Un logon ka support use karein jo aapke favour mein hain.
S|Devils: Jo log oppose kar rahe hain, unke opposition ko silence karein.
S|Consigliore: Top management mein ek trusted advisor ka support secure karein.
H2|Principle 2: Build Execution into Strategy through Fair Process
P|Execution ke dauran logon ke andar trust aur voluntary cooperation, yani apni marzi se saath dene ki willingness, badhane ke liye Fair Process zaroori hai.
B|Fair Process: Iske teen "E Principles" hote hain.
S|Engagement: Un sabhi logon ko decision-making mein involve karna jinpar decision ka effect padega.
S|Explanation: Logon ko clearly samjhana ki decisions kyun liye gaye hain.
S|Expectation Clarity: Strategy set hone ke baad naye rules aur expectations ko bilkul clear tareeqe se batana.

P|Naye market spaces, Value Innovation aur ek systematic framework ke saath companies traditional raste se hatkar ek behtar aur bada future bana sakti hain.
