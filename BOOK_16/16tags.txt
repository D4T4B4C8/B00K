H1|Level 5 Leadership

B|Definition: Transition ke samay sabhi good-to-great companies me Level 5 Leadership thi. Level 5 leaders ek anokha paradox hote hain. Unme Personal Humility, yani vyaktigat namrata, aur Professional Will, yani mazboot professional sankalp, dono ka behtareen mel hota hai.

H2|The 5-Level Hierarchy

B|Level 1: Highly Capable Individual — Apne talent aur achhi work habits se contribution deta hai.

B|Level 2: Contributing Team Member — Team ke goals achieve karne me madad karta hai.

B|Level 3: Competent Manager — Logon aur resources ko organize karta hai.

B|Level 4: Effective Leader — Ek clear vision ke piche logon ko motivate karta hai.

B|Level 5: Level 5 Executive — Humility aur professional will ke through lambe samay tak chalne wali greatness laata hai.

B|Examples: Colman Mockler, David Maxwell, Darwin Smith, Joe Cullman aur Fred Allen.
