B="Mindset: Carol S. Dweck ki Research"
A="Carol S. Dweck"
H1|Carol S. Dweck ki "Mindset" Research aur Introduction
B|Mindset ka Power: Carol S. Dweck ne 20 saal ki research ke baad bataya ki humare beliefs, yani vishwaas, humari life ko bahut gehrai se badal sakte hain. Aapka mindset, yani aap khud ko kaise dekhte hain, yeh tay karta hai ki aap logon aur events ke saath kaise respond karte hain.
B|Hallmark of Growth Mindset: Jab situations bilkul theek na chal rahi hon, tab bhi khud ko stretch karna, seekhne ki koshish karna aur lage rehna growth mindset ki main pehchan hai.

H1|Growth vs Fixed Mindset: Definitions aur Approaches
B|Fixed Mindset kya hai?: Yeh ek aisa belief hai jisme aap sochte hain ki aapki qualities aur abilities permanent hain aur badli nahi ja sakti. Is mindset ke logon ko lagta hai ki unhe baar-baar khud ko smart aur sahi prove karna zaroori hai.
B|Growth Mindset kya hai?: Yeh belief hai ki aapki qualities aur abilities ko effort, experience aur practice se badla aur better banaya ja sakta hai. Is mindset ke log challenges se darrte nahi, balki unmein achha perform karte hain aur grow karte hain.
B|Learning vs Validation: Dono mindsets success ko alag tareeke se dekhte hain.
S|Fixed Mindset: Aise log success ko perfect hona, smart dikhna ya hamesha sahi hona samajhte hain. Unka main focus khud ko validate, yani sahi aur capable prove karne par hota hai.
S|Growth Mindset: Aise log tab tak khud ko successful samajhte hain jab tak woh seekh rahe hain, improve kar rahe hain aur grow kar rahe hain.
S|Bachpan aur Learning: Bachpan mein hum bina zyada darr ke seekhte hain. Lekin bade hone par hum doosron ke saamne smart dikhne ke chakkar mein naye challenges se darrne lagte hain.
B|Stretching vs Certainty: Dono mindsets comfort aur challenges ko alag tareeke se dekhte hain.
S|Growth Mindset: Aise log naye aur mushkil challenges pasand karte hain aur un logon ke saath rehna chahte hain jo unhe push karke better banne mein madad karein.
S|Fixed Mindset: Aise log tab zyada comfortable hote hain jab woh apne comfort zone mein hon aur results predictable hon. Woh un logon ke saath rehna pasand karte hain jo unhe apne baare mein achha feel karayein.

H1|Ability vs Learning: Potential ko Pehchanna
B|Potential ki Sachai: Kisi bhi insaan ka potential sirf ek test score ya assessment se decide nahi kiya ja sakta.
B|Limitations: Fixed mindset wale log aksar test scores ke base par khud ko limit kar lete hain. Growth mindset wale log samajhte hain ki unki limit unki seekhne ki ichha aur effort se judi hoti hai. Isi wajah se aam dikhne wale bachche bhi aage chal kar outstanding artists, scientists aur sportspeople ban sakte hain.
B|Potential ko Identify karne ke Examples:
S|Marina Semyonova: Russian dancer aur teacher Marina Semyonova trial period mein students ki sirf physical ability par focus nahi karti thi. Woh dekhti thi ki students feedback ko kaise handle karte hain.
S|NASA: NASA un candidates ko prefer karti hai jo bade failures ke baad bounce back, yani dobara khade hokar aage badhe hain, un logon ke comparison mein jinka record sirf smooth success se bhara raha hai.
B|Self-Assessment: Growth mindset wale log apni abilities ka assessment zyada accurately kar sakte hain, kyunki unhe apne ego ko satisfy karne ke liye khud ko bada-chadhakar dikhane ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

H1|Failure aur Setbacks ke Prati Attitude
B|Blame vs Learning: Jab cheezein galat hoti hain, toh fixed aur growth mindset dono wale log bura feel karte hain. Lekin unka response alag hota hai.
B|Fixed Mindset Response: Aise log failure ko apni permanent identity bana lete hain.
S|"Main ek failure hoon."
S|Woh apni kamiyaan chhupane ke liye doosron ko blame karte hain ya excuses banate hain. Kabhi-kabhi woh depressed hokar haar maan lete hain.
B|Growth Mindset Response: Aise log failure ko ek temporary problem ya incident maante hain jise effort se improve kiya ja sakta hai.
S|"Main is baar fail hua hoon."
S|Woh apni kamiyon ko analyze karte hain aur naye tareeke dhundhte hain.
B|College Study: Ek study mein dekha gaya ki failure ke baad growth mindset wale students un logon se seekhte the jinhone unse better perform kiya tha. Fixed mindset wale students apna ego bachane ke liye un logon ko dekhte the jinhone unse bhi kharab perform kiya tha.

H1|Negative Labels aur Stereotypes
B|Fixed Mindset aur Stereotypes: Fixed mindset wale log negative stereotypes, jaise "ladkiyan math aur science mein achhi nahi hoti", se jaldi demotivate ho sakte hain aur drop out kar sakte hain.
B|Growth Mindset aur Stereotypes: Growth mindset wale log in stereotypes ko sirf doosron ka ek nazariya samajhte hain aur apna confidence khoye bina better perform karte hain.

H1|Talent vs Effort
B|The Tortoise and the Hare Fable: Fixed mindset wale log aksar sochte hain ki agar unhe kisi cheez ke liye bahut effort karna pad raha hai, toh iska matlab hai ki unmein natural talent ki kami hai. Woh mehnat karne se bhi darr sakte hain kyunki unhe lagta hai ki agar effort ke baad bhi result na mila, toh unka special dikhne ka image toot jayega.
B|Effort ki Real Value: Mozart ne bhi apna pehla bada piece compose karne se pehle 10 saal ki kadi mehnat ki thi. Inventions aksar kisi ek achanak flash of brilliance ka result nahi hote, balki lagatar ki gayi koshishon ka nateeja hote hain.
B|Desire to Win: Fixed mindset wale log aksar sirf kisi specific result, jaise jeet ya award, ke liye effort karte hain. Growth mindset wale log excellence ke passion se kaam karte hain aur result unke kaam ka by-product ban jata hai.

H1|Mindset in Parenting aur Education
H2|Messages about Success
B|Don't Praise for Ability or Talent: Bachchon ki intelligence ya smartness ki baar-baar tareef karna unmein fixed mindset paida kar sakta hai. Isse un par har baar perfect dikhne ka pressure aa sakta hai.
S|Research: Research ke mutabiq jab smartness ke liye praise kiye gaye bachche fail hote hain, toh unka performance kharab ho sakta hai aur lagbhag 40 percent bachchon ne apne scores ke baare mein jhooth bola.
B|Praise for Process: Bachchon ki koshish, strategies, choices aur tenacity, yani lagatar lage rehne ki quality, ki tareef karein. Isse woh naye challenges lene ke liye motivated rehte hain.

H2|Messages about Failure
B|Negative Labels se Bachein: Bachchon ko negative labels, jaise "useless", na dein. Unhe failure se bachane ke liye faltu excuses bhi na banayein.
B|Constructive Feedback: Unhe aisa feedback dein jisse woh apni galti samajh saken aur seekh saken.
B|Punishment ka Asar: Bachchon ko punishment dena kabhi-kabhi ulta result de sakta hai. Unhe respect ke saath decision-making ka process samjhana chahiye.
S|Agar woh effort nahi daal rahe hain, toh unhe gently bataya ja sakta hai ki aap unke effort ki kami se disappointed hain.

H2|Teaching, Coaching aur Education
B|Great Teachers: Achhe teachers standards ko kam nahi karte. Woh high standards ke saath students ko wahan tak pahunchne ke liye tools aur support bhi dete hain.
B|Marva Collins ka Example: Marva Collins ne un bachchon ko transform kiya jinhone har school mein haar maan li thi. Unhone unhe challenging material aur bahut zyada vishwaas diya.
B|Bobby Knight: Bobby Knight ko Fixed Mindset Coach ke example ke roop mein dikhaya gaya hai. Woh sirf results par focus karte the aur players ko publicly shame karte the, jisse unka passion kam ho jata tha.
B|John Wooden: John Wooden ko Growth Mindset Coach ke example ke roop mein dikhaya gaya hai. Unhone team se kabhi mistake-free game ki demand nahi ki. Woh sabhi players ko barabar respect dete the aur unhe har baar apna personal best dene ke liye push karte the.

H1|Business aur Leadership mein Mindset

H1|Relationships mein Mindset
H2|Love Relationships
B|Mindset ka Asar: Mindset yeh shape karta hai ki hum apne baare mein, apne partner ke baare mein aur relationship ki qualities ke baare mein kya beliefs rakhte hain.
B|Fixed Mindset in Relationships:
S|Aise log sochte hain ki agar do log ek-doosre ke liye bane hain, toh woh bina bole ek-doosre ki baat samajh jayenge. Isse mind-reading ki expectation paida hoti hai.
S|Woh sochte hain ki partners ko har baat par hamesha agree karna chahiye.
S|Relationship mein problem aane par woh partner ko blame karte hain ya khud ko "unloveable" label kar dete hain.
S|Woh apne partner ke saath compete aur compare bhi karte hain.
B|Growth Mindset in Relationships:
S|Aise log believe karte hain ki partners aur relationships dono badal sakte hain aur naye positive changes la sakte hain.
S|Woh differences ko kam karne ke liye communication aur effort ka use karte hain.

H2|Friendship
